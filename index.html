💡 Good to know

💵 Cash

Island ATMs charge about €6 a go. Lipe's can run dry, so arrive with cash for all five nights. Park fees and longtails are cash only.

👕 Temple clothes

Shoulders and knees covered, nothing tight or see-through. Shoes off inside the halls.

🚕 Bangkok transport

Grab or Bolt for taxis. The BTS Skytrain and MRT beat the traffic. River boats are cheap and the nicest ride in town.

🐒 Monkeys and lizards

Never feed them. Hide food and plastic bags at Monkey Beach and in Lanta's national park. Give Lumphini's monitors a few metres.

🌊 Sea days

Reef-safe sunscreen, a rash vest, and travel-sickness tablets for the long speedboat rides.

🛵 Scooters

Bring an International Driving Permit, wear the helmet, and make sure your insurance covers scooters.

📱 Phone

Set up an eSIM (AIS, TrueMove H or dtac) before you fly, so you have data when you land.

📅 Holidays

5 Dec is Father's Day (it may also be observed on Mon 7 Dec) and 10 Dec is Constitution Day. Expect crowds and some closures.

🔌 Plugs and water

EU two-pin plugs usually fit, but pack an adapter. Drink bottled or filtered water, not tap.
